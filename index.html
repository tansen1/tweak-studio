About

The studio

Genres come after music — they're labels applied to what already exists. We start with the brand, not a reference track.

We build a brand's sonic language from the ground up: melody, rhythm, and harmony define its motifs; instrumentation, synthesis, and sound design give them texture; production, mixing, and mastering shape how they live across every touchpoint. The same way a visual identity makes a brand recognisable before you read its name, we believe its sound should make it recognisable before you see the screen. We design identity. Our medium is sound.
